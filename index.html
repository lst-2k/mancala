MANCALA

How to play Mancala

Goal: collect more marbles in your store (the big pit at the bottom) than your opponent.
  • Your side: the six pits down the left column. Your opponent has the right column, and their store is the big pit at the top.
  • Your move: tap one of your glowing pits. All its marbles are picked up and dropped one by one in each pit going anticlockwise (down your column, into your store at the bottom, then up your opponent’s column).
  • Skipping: you drop into your own store but skip your opponent’s store.
  • Extra turn: if your last marble lands in your own store, you go again.
  • Capture: if your last marble lands in an empty pit on your side, and the pit opposite has marbles, you capture both piles into your store.
  • Game end: when one side has no marbles left, the other player keeps all the marbles remaining on their side. Most marbles in the store wins.
Tips: plan moves that finish exactly in your store for extra turns, and watch for captures on both sides.
Modes: play the computer (Easy, Medium, Hard), or online with a friend using a shared 4-letter room code.
